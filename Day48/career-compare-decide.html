🔬 How this was researched ▼

💰 Salary & Job Demand

Salary figures were pulled from BLS OEWS May 2024 (median base wages), Glassdoor 2025–26 (total compensation averages from 700k+ submissions), Levels.fyi End of Year 2025 (base + equity + bonus at large tech), and job-posting aggregators (Indeed, Built In). BLS and Glassdoor diverge significantly for SE (~$133K vs ~$151K) because BLS captures base wage across all employers including small firms and government, while Glassdoor skews toward tech-sector employers that pay equity. The table uses BLS median base as a conservative anchor. Job demand scores weight both current open positions and BLS projected growth through 2034.

⚠ Conflict resolved: BLS reports SE median at $133,080; Glassdoor reports $150,591 total comp. These are not contradictory — different measurement scopes. The salary column in this tool uses BLS median base as the primary anchor for apples-to-apples comparison.

⏱ Time & Cost to Transition

Transition timeline estimates are drawn from Course Report bootcamp data, DEV Community practitioner articles (2026), and MentorCruise career switch guides. Software Engineering requires the most technical reskilling: a 3–6 month intensive bootcamp or 6–12 months self-directed learning is typical before job-readiness. Data Science often requires a master's degree or equivalent (1–2 years) for competitive roles, though analyst-entry roles can be reached via bootcamp in 6–9 months. Product Management is the fastest lateral transition for professionals with domain expertise — 3–6 months of PM-specific learning and portfolio building suffices. UX Design bootcamps run 3–6 months; the 2026 market is crowded at junior level. Costs are approximate (SE/DS bootcamps $10–20K; UX bootcamps $5–15K; PM courses typically $500–3K plus self-study).

⚠ Transition difficulty is highly background-dependent. A software engineer switching to DS faces a shorter path than a teacher switching to SE. The scores here assume a typical non-technical professional career switcher.

📈 Career Growth & Long-Term Stability

Growth scores combine BLS projected employment growth through 2034 (SE: 15%; DS: 36%; Management/PM: 6–10%; Web/UX Designers: 7%), ceiling salary trajectories from Levels.fyi (SE Staff median $457K; DS FAANG $200K–$450K; VP Product $250K+; UX Lead/Director $150K–$250K), and industry signals about AI's impact on each role. DS and SE in AI/ML specializations have the strongest tailwinds. PM growth has slowed (hiring down 14% in 2025–26 per Ravio) but compensation for existing PMs rose 5.2%. UX is stable but junior hiring remains soft.

🚧 Entry Barrier & Competition

Entry barrier scores are inverted for this criterion — a higher score means lower barrier / less competition, which is better for a career switcher. SE entry in 2026 is described by multiple practitioners as the hardest it's ever been at junior level, with companies using AI for boilerplate work (Dev.to, Extern.com, Feb–Jun 2026). DS roles face a skills mismatch problem — many candidates lack the right balance of coding, statistics, and domain knowledge (Cloudurable 2025). PM is the most lateral-friendly role for experienced professionals without a technical background (Medium/Aakash Gupta, Jun 2025; invinciblemoth.com). UX junior market is crowded but mid-level UX has stronger demand per MentorCruise 2026.

⚠ Note: "Entry barrier" for a career switcher is different from raw competition. PM has a lower credential barrier but requires strong portfolio/case study work and networking. The score reflects overall accessibility for a motivated non-technical career switcher.
