What this tool is for

This Playbook stores reusable AI workflows — prompt templates you fill in and reuse — instead of one-off prompts you retype every time. Everything is saved locally in this browser, nothing leaves your machine.

  • Workflows — your saved templates, organized by task type. Fill in the blanks, copy, paste into Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a new prompt from labeled building blocks (role, objective, constraints...) with a live preview.
  • Loop Builder — wrap any prompt so the AI keeps iterating on its own until a goal or stop condition is met.
  • Settings — theme, default model, import/export your data, keyboard shortcuts.

What is the AI Playbook?

The Playbook is a personal, offline library of AI prompt workflows — reusable, fill-in-the-blank templates — plus two tools for building new prompts from scratch. It's built for engineers who bounce between Claude, ChatGPT, and other tools and don't want to reconstruct context every time.

  • Dashboard — your overview: stats, favorites, and what to jump back into.
  • Workflows — saved prompt templates grouped by task (debugging, code review, architecture, automation, learning, cross-tool handoff). Fill variables, copy, done.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a one-shot prompt from labeled blocks: Role, Objective, Context, Constraints, Reasoning Strategy, Output Format, Tone, Examples, Quality Checks.
  • Loop Builder — wrap a prompt so the AI iterates on its own: Goal, Evaluation Criteria, Improvement Strategy, Stop Conditions, Safety Rules.
  • Settings — theme, default model, export/import your data, and shortcuts.

    • All data is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere.
