    Control Tower Briefing

    Alerts appear as disruptions hit your network. Each one has a countdown — act before it expires or it resolves itself, badly.

    Reading an alert

    • Priority badge tells you urgency: High, Medium, Low
    • The description tells you what's actually happening — read it, the right fix depends on it
    • The impact line shows which KPIs are on the line

    Taking action

    • Pick the response that best matches the situation — one option is always the strongest fit
    • A mismatched action still resolves the alert, but barely helps
    • Ignore closes the alert immediately with no fix — expect fallout
    • Delay Decision buys time now, but the consequence lands a few seconds later
    • Letting the timer hit zero is treated as a worst-case outcome

    Scoring

    • Strong decisions raise KPIs, protect revenue, and add to your score
    • Weak decisions do the opposite, and raise operating cost
    • Your final grade blends KPI health with operating cost efficiency
