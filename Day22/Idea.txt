1. Startup Idea: An AI-powered Job and Career Intelligence Platform that uses autonomous AI agents to help job seekers in India navigate the GenAI job market. It automatically scans job listings, matches skills to roles, generates tailored resumes and cover letters, and provides real-time upskilling roadmaps based on current market demand for AI/ML, Prompt Engineering, and Agentic AI roles.
2. Problem Being Solved: The GenAI job market is growing fast but most job seekers don't know which AI skills to learn, which roles to target, or how to position themselves. People rely on scattered LinkedIn posts, YouTube tutorials, and generic job boards. There is no single intelligent system that connects skill gaps to learning paths to job matching to application automation. This is extremely painful for freshers and mid-level professionals trying to break into AI roles.
3. Target Customers: B2C — Indian job seekers aged 21 to 35 including final-year students targeting AI roles, working professionals wanting to pivot into GenAI, and early-career developers upskilling into Agentic AI or LLM Ops. Secondary B2B are HR teams at Indian tech companies hiring for AI roles.
4. Why I Want to Build It: I am actively navigating the AI job market myself and experiencing firsthand how broken and overwhelming the process is. My proximity to the problem and deep interest in GenAI and autonomous agents gives me authentic founder-market fit and an unfair advantage in understanding user pain.
5. Existing Validation: Pre-validation stage. Strong market signals exist including explosive growth in Prompt Engineer and AI Agent Developer job postings on LinkedIn India, communities like IndiaAI with 50,000 plus members seeking career guidance, and personal conversations with 10 plus peers facing identical challenges.
6. Target Market: India as primary launch market covering Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Pune. Expansion to Southeast Asia and Indian diaspora in US and UK within 18 months.
7. Project Type: Starting as a side project with intent to transition to a full-time bootstrapped startup within 6 months if early traction is validated. Open to VC funding at Series A once product-market fit is established.
