# Day 23 — Build Your Customer & MVP Blueprint
**#60DayClaudeChallenge**

## 📌 Overview
Day 23 focused on a core startup truth: **great products are built around customer needs, not founder assumptions.** Using Claude, I turned my Day 22 Startup Validation Report into a complete **Customer & MVP Blueprint** covering customer discovery, MVP strategy, feature prioritization, and a focused 30-day execution plan.

**Project analyzed:** AI Career Intelligence Platform — an autonomous skill-gap diagnosis → upskilling roadmap → AI-tailored application tool for Prompt Engineering, AI/ML & Agentic AI roles in India.

## 🧩 Customer Insights

| Section | Key Takeaway |
|---|---|
| **ICP** | 22–29 yo, B.Tech/B.E., SDE-1/2 or QA at services/product companies, 0–3 yrs exp, ₹5–12 LPA, in Tier-I cities expanding to Tier-II, trying to break into AI/ML, Prompt Engineering, or Agentic AI roles |
| **Persona** | "Rohan Sharma, 26 — The Frustrated Upskiller": bookmarks 40+ tutorials, has no structured plan, burned by a ₹4,999 bootcamp with zero ROI, triggered by a friend landing an ₹18 LPA Agentic AI role |
| **Biggest pain point** | Doesn't know which AI skills *actually* matter right now → decision paralysis, made worse by fragmented advice across LinkedIn/YouTube/word-of-mouth |
| **Biggest objection** | "Isn't this just ChatGPT with a UI?" — the product has to *prove* it's a closed loop (live data + tracked roadmap + tailored output), not just content |
| **Strongest buying trigger** | A peer publicly landing an AI role on LinkedIn → instant FOMO-driven search for "how do I do that too" |

## 🚀 MVP Recommendations

**Build first:**
- Skill-gap diagnostic quiz → personalised roadmap (manually-assisted is fine to start)
- 10–15 hand-crafted tailored resume/cover-letter samples
- Landing page + waitlist
- 2–3 pricing concepts tested directly with real users

**Don't build yet:**
- Fully autonomous auto-apply (trust risk — needs human review first)
- Deep ATS/job-board integrations
- B2B HR/recruiter product
- Native mobile app

**MoSCoW in one line:** *Must* = diagnostic + roadmap + waitlist + pricing test → *Should* = job-board feed + progress tracking → *Could* = automation + community → *Won't* = auto-apply + B2B + mobile app (for now).

**Success metric that matters most:** ≥15% of activated users willing to pay (deposit/pre-order) — this is the real go/no-go signal, not signups.

## 💡 Key Learnings
- **Pain existing ≠ willingness to pay.** The report repeatedly flagged that the problem is well-validated, but *paying* for a solution vs. using free ChatGPT/YouTube is the actual open question — and that's exactly where my 30-day plan needs to focus.
- **MVP scope discipline is harder than MVP building.** The "Don't Build" list (auto-apply, deep integrations, mobile app) was as valuable as the "Build First" list — it stopped me from over-engineering before validating demand.
- **Objections reveal hidden pain points.** "Isn't this just ChatGPT with a UI?" isn't just an objection — it's a positioning problem I need to solve in the product itself, not just in marketing copy.
- **Real-world application:** I now have a concrete 30-day customer-discovery plan (20–25 interviews, smoke-test MVP, real payment ask) instead of jumping straight into building.
