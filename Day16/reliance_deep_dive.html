Business Overview

Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) is India's largest conglomerate by revenue and market cap. Founded by Dhirubhai Ambani in 1958 and incorporated in 1973, it operates across five key verticals: Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) — the world's largest refinery complex at Jamnagar; Digital Services (Jio) — 524M+ subscribers, India's largest telecom; Retail — India's largest retailer (₹3.7L Cr revenue); Media & Entertainment — via JioStar (Disney Hotstar merger); and Oil & Gas E&P. In FY26, consumer businesses (Jio + Retail + Media) contributed over 55% of consolidated EBITDA for the first time, marking completion of RIL's transformation from an energy company to a diversified consumer conglomerate.

Valuation Verdict

On a simple P/E basis, RIL looks expensive vs sector peers. However, the conglomerate structure means each business (Jio alone could be worth ₹6–8L Cr) deserves a sum-of-parts (SOTP) lens. On EV/EBITDA, valuation is closer to fair. The Jio IPO, when it happens, could crystallise significant embedded value. At CMP, the market is pricing in a transformation story — not just current earnings.

Valuation: FAIR-TO-EXPENSIVE on earnings; FAIR on SOTP basis.

Key Concern: FCF Compression

RIL's high capex (₹1.44L Cr in FY26 vs ₹1.31L Cr in FY25) has been a key reason for free cash flow (FCF) being constrained. This drove significant underperformance in the stock over FY25–FY26. However, Motilal Oswal estimates that capex has likely peaked and RIL could generate ~₹1 trillion cumulative FCF over FY24–27, which would be a major re-rating catalyst.

Watch-Points (Risks)
!
ROE/ROCE Compression
ROE has fallen from 24% (FY23) to 8.8% (FY26) — well below the 15%+ threshold. Heavy equity dilution and capex are the cause, but this metric needs recovery for re-rating.
!
Free Cash Flow (FCF) Pressure
Capex of ₹1.44L Cr in FY26 is consuming operating cash flows. FCF has been the primary reason for stock underperformance (-9.1% in 1 year). If capex doesn't moderate, this risk persists.
!
O2C Segment Vulnerability
Q4 FY26 PAT fell 12.6% YoY largely due to O2C weakness (geopolitics, freight/insurance costs, GRM volatility). ~38% of EBITDA still tied to global commodity cycles.
!
Expensive Headline P/E
At 40× P/E vs sector average of 17–18×, any earnings disappointment or macro disruption could lead to significant de-rating.

Overall Fundamental Quality

⚡ MODERATE-TO-STRONG

RIL is an undeniably powerful franchise — India's largest company with diversified, growing businesses and zero promoter pledging. The transformation toward consumer-led earnings (55%+ EBITDA from Jio/Retail in FY26) is a positive structural shift. However, ROE below 10%, heavy capex, FCF compression, and a demanding P/E multiple make the near-term risk-reward nuanced. The Jio IPO is the key optionality event. Long-term structural thesis remains intact; short-term is dependent on FCF inflection and O2C margin recovery.
